## Decisions and notes

Step 2, locales
- Every locale file is flat: a SpeechLine key maps to a template. The formatter (`src/i18n/format.ts`) speaks one ICU subset: `{name}` and `{n, plural, one {# second} other {# seconds}}` (CLDR categories per language, `=0` exact branches, `#` is the number). No escaping, so a text never holds a literal brace. Spanish needs only one and other; Polish and Russian need one, few, many, other.
- `src/i18n/catalog.ts` is the one list of keys (`requiredKeys()`): 20 cue ids, 21 lesson ids in ladder order with their line counts, the free keys, and the survey keys derived from `src/survey/defs.ts`. test/locales.test.ts holds all four files to that list, in that order, and to the documented arguments (the `ARGUMENTS` table there is the interface the lesson, menu and delivery code must fill).
- Lesson ids for step 8 (module): ears-headphones, ears-glossary, ears-walls, ears-shapes, ears-game (0); hover-height, hover-turn (1); move-speed, move-stop (2); walls-hall, walls-pillar, walls-row (3); corridor (4); rings-first, rings-line, rings-small, rings-course (5); shapes-square, shapes-series, shapes-cube (6); free-garden (7). Each lesson has `title`, `intro.n`, `goal.n`, `pass`, `retry`, `hint.1`. A lesson file must use these ids and keys, and pass the parameters the text names (hover-height.goal.1 takes seconds, move-stop.intro.1 takes metres). The fast path flags are the lesson files' business.
- Key letters are never written into lesson texts: the help, recording, sending and survey lines take `{key}` (and `{skip}`) from the host, so rebinding works. Directions `dir.<sector>` are stand-alone phrases ("On your left.") spoken before a preview.
- Surveys are TypeScript data in `src/survey/defs.ts` (first flight 9 items, returning pilot 10, child 6). The workload scales are 0 to 9, not 0 to 10, so that one key press answers; convert with value * 100 / 9.
- The speech lint (`tools/locale-lint.ts`) refuses empty or padded texts, more than 240 characters, characters a synthesiser reads badly, emoji, Latin words in Russian texts outside a short name list, Cyrillic in other languages, a plural block without its language's categories, and a text copied from English. Each rule has a negative control in the test.

Step 3, analytic worlds
- `createAnalyticWorld(spec)` (`src/world/analytic-world.ts`) returns `AnalyticWorldInfo`: the contract's AnalyticWorld plus `resolved` (every parameter, defaults filled), `start` (Pose), `floorY` (0) and `landmarks` (named points, so lessons place checkpoints and zones without computing coordinates). `makeSpec(generator, params, seed)` gives a complete spec at the current version. A spec with an unknown generator, parameter, value out of range, wrong version or a layout that does not fit throws `WorldSpecError` with the reason.
- Digest = SHA-256 of the canonical JSON (sorted keys, no spaces, `src/util/canonical.ts`) of the resolved spec `{generator, params, seed, version}`; omitted and explicit defaults are one spec. The id is `<generator>-<8 hex>`, never bare hex. The digest covers the spec only, so test/world.test.ts also pins a hash of each world's objects and of its distance field at 600 fixed points: change a generator, a default or a draw order and those fail until GENERATOR_VERSION is bumped and the goldens regenerated.
- Shapes (src/world/parts.ts header): ring `diameter` is the CLEAR opening and `tube` the tube RADIUS; square and cube-frame `side` is the clear opening and `bar` the bar thickness (outer side = side + 2 bar); pillar `height` is centred on `pos`. A gate faces local -Z for a 'forward' pass; `yawQuat(yaw)` makes the quaternion. Zones are not solid and carry material 'floor' and order 0.
- Generators and their landmarks: open (floor, optional ceiling, optional stop zone: start); hall (wall-front/back/left/right at start height); pillars (single, row, scatter; hall 40 x 60 x 12); corridor (5 x 4 m; layouts straight, obstacle, bends, jogs, full = 100 m straight, pillar, 6 left-right bends, 4 up-down jogs, 6 bends with pillars; landmarks section-straight, -obstacle, -bends, -jogs, -mixed and end); rings, squares, cube-frames (hall 60 x 160 x 20; layouts single with offsets, line, turn, course; `sizes` gives a size per gate; ids ring-N, square-N, cube-N; order 0..). Hall-based worlds start near the +Z end and look along -Z. Everything is seeded by `createRng` (mulberry32); the order of draws is part of the version.
- Rays: sphere tracing (64 exact steps, then at least 5 cm steps with bisection, so a ray skimming a wall still finds its true first hit); 30 000 rays cost 20 to 360 ms here. A ray from inside a solid hits at distance 0. Material: objects are 'obstacle'; the room's surfaces are floor, ceiling or wall by normal. `voxelM` is 0.1 by default (a `voxelM` parameter).
- Mutation check done by hand: stepping the tracer by 2 d fails 20 tests.

Step 4, scene sampler
- `createSceneSampler(options).sample(flightSample, world | null, frame = 'heading')` returns a `SceneSample`: ten `SectorReading`s in SECTORS order (30 rays: the axis and two rays 20 degrees to either side), the voiced `hazards` (at most `maxVoices`, 0 to 3, most urgent first, each with sector, rank, urgency, distance, ttcS, dir, material, objectId) and `meanFreeDistanceM` for the room layer. Directions are in the listener frame (heading: yaw only; body: full attitude). One sampler per craft; hysteresis and stickiness remember the previous call; `reset()` on a new lesson or a seek (a clock that runs backwards is also handled).
- A sector's distance is the minimum over its rays (to the hull, `bodyRadiusM` 0.12), its time to collision the soonest over its rays, and its direction, material and object those of its most threatening ray. Time to collision is to the SURFACE: hull distance along the hit normal over the speed toward it, so flying along a wall has none whichever ray sees it (without a normal the line of sight stands in). A line-of-sight time was tried first and failed the corridor test: at 45 m/s the oblique rays at the side walls outranked the end wall.
- Risk = max(enterDist / distance, enterTtc / ttc): 1 at the threshold. On at risk >= 1 at once; off when risk < releaseRatio (0.8) and holdMs (300) have passed since it last reached 1. Ranking = risk x sector weight (ahead over behind) x stickiness (1.25) for a voice already sounding. All defaults are in `DEFAULT_SAMPLER_CONFIG` and are starting values for pilot tests: enter distances front 6 m, diagonals 5, front-up 3, front-down 1.8, left and right 4, back 3, up and down 1.5; enter time 3 s. A low hover (under 1.6 m) makes the floor voice sound.
- Data for the engine (step 6): hazard `ttcS` null means use `distance` for the pulse period; `material` picks the safety timbre (obstacle for pillars and gates); `sector` is a stable voice key.

Step 5, tracks, themes, prefs
- Shared toolkit `src/util/check.ts`: validators return `Validation<T>` = the value or ALL issues `{path, message}` (`formatIssues` joins them); `src/util/canonical.ts` has `canonicalPretty` (sorted keys, two spaces, short number arrays inline) for every data file the engine writes, so a file written twice is byte-equal. Writers add one final newline.
- Themes (`src/theme/`): `LAYER_PARAMS` in schema.ts is THE list of numbers each layer takes, with range, default and meaning; a file with a missing or unknown number, a value out of range, a note index beyond the scale or a pair out of order is refused with its path. Parameters named *Step are NOTE INDICES of the theme's scale (0 = root), never frequencies. `themes/soft|clear|race.json` share one scale (C major pentatonic from C3 = 130.8128 Hz, 4 octaves, 21 notes), limiter ceiling -6 dBFS, fade-in 1 s, duck 12 dB, and are written by `writeTheme` (a test holds the files to canonical form). `getBuiltinTheme(id)`, `resolveTheme(id, custom)`. The engine must make every pitch with `noteHz(scale, index)`, `heightNoteIndex` (polarity), `gateMelody` (a rising run that the last gate completes) and `shiftNote` (the pilot's `layerPitchSteps`, in scale steps); `isInScale` is the test of harmony. `applyCalm(theme)` (data: `CALM_PRESET`) when `prefs.calm`: quieter, slower, less rough, duller, no pitch moves.
- Spatial audio, the owner's question of 2026-11-07 ("the best developments, the order of the series"): already analysed in docs/research/01 (section 1, 9). Decision kept: direct per-source HRTF is the reference (built-in PannerNode now; later own ConvolverNode or AudioWorklet engine with SADIE II or HUTUBS sets); Ambisonics only as the ambience bed, order 3 (4 at most on one 32-channel bus; 5 and more need two buses and have no evidence of benefit). The theme parameter `room.bedOrder` (0 to 4, default 0) carries that choice; the first engine renders order 0.
- Prefs (`src/prefs/`): `defaultPrefs()`, strict `validatePrefs`, lenient `readPrefs` for stored values (missing field = default, a bad field or a bad layer entry is replaced or dropped and reported, never throws; a fuzz test holds it), `updatePrefs`, `writePrefs`. Pitch offsets are in scale steps. Defaults are the spec's: soft, not calm, up is higher, self-voice on, captions on, motor sound off, full path, heading frame.
- Tracks (`src/track/`): `validateTrack`/`parseTrack`/`writeTrack`/`trackDigest`; gates are ring, square or cube-frame; version n > 1 must have the parent {same id, n - 1}; version 1 has no parent or a parent in another track (a fork). `checkSuccession(prev, next)` and `TrackLibrary.add` refuse a published version with other content ("never edited in place"), a skipped number, a wrong parent, an earlier date, another scene and a no-change "new version". `trackToObjects(track, {transform, solid})` maps the scan-frame gates through T(p) = s p + t (sizes times s); gates are not solid by default (a scan's collider is its own walls). `scanToFlown`, `flownToScan`, `shapeToFlown`.

Step 6, engine
- Two halves. `src/cues/` is DOM-free (in tsconfig.core): `rules.ts` (pulse period from time to collision, distance code, Doppler, elevation pitch code, the +7.5 degree HRTF elevation fix, roughness, tempo, target rate and aim miss, room, soft-clip curve, compressor make-up, fade curve), `schedule.ts` (lookahead pulse trains), `voices.ts` (the 8 + 24 allocator, priorities safety > target > shape > previews), `shape.ts` (traces), `planner.ts` (`CuePlanner`: a SonicFrameInput in, a `FramePlan` with voices, height, speed, room, urgency and the `ActiveCue` list out). `src/audio/` is the only DOM code: `engine.ts`, `synth.ts` (timbres), `output.ts` (limiter chain and fade), `speech.ts`. Replay (step 10) can re-derive cues with a `CuePlanner` alone.
- Host loop: `createSonicEngine(options)` returns `FlyBySoundEngine` (contract SonicEngine plus `setLocale`, `planner`, `stats()`, `fadeIn()`, `context`, `speaking`, `pump()`). `start(ac)` inside the Start gesture; `update({sample, world, target, floorY})` every frame; `cue(e)` for touch, gate, lap, checkpoint, reset, fanfare and previews (glossary); `say(line)` speaks and captions (`options.caption(text, line)` and `onSpeech`), ducking every other layer by `duckDb`; `activeCues()` for FlightFrame.c; `auxInput` for the motor sound. Options: theme, prefs, layers, locale, dicts, speech (default the browser's speechSynthesis; null = captions only), sampler, pump (the engine's own 25 ms wake-up, on for a live context), testHooks (the proofs' negative controls only).
- ActiveCue ids: `safety.<material>:<sector>` for hazards, `<cue>:preview` for previews, otherwise the cue id (`target.hum`, `shape.ring`, `height.tone` or `height.rough`, `speed.tick`, `room.bed`, one-off notes).
- Numbers in code, not in themes (starting values): urgent hazard = time to collision under 1.5 s or distance under 1 m, then the other layers duck 9 dB and safety is never ducked; level slope 4 dB per doubling down to -18 dB; ILD boost under 2 m; Doppler held to 60 cents; off course on the hum = beating up to 7 Hz; the shape layer traces the next target or the nearest solid object within 12 m; aim by velocity above 1 m/s; the gate melody starts on note 5 (C4) and climbs one octave.
- Sound design measured, not only chosen: every spatial note has a sub-octave (low-frequency energy) and a quiet contact transient or air band at 2-6 kHz, because the first version had almost nothing above 2 kHz and the HRTF had no level or pinna cues to work with (3 dB between the ears at 90 degrees); now 18 dB above 2 kHz and 0.81 ms arrival difference below 800 Hz. Held pad tones swirl 0.3 Hz instead of a 5-cent chorus, whose 1 Hz beating could pass for the off-course cue.
- Fixed in step 4 code: a wall seen by all three rays of a sector took a side ray 20 degrees off (equal risks differed by the tracer's rounding); ties within 1e-4 now go to the nearest ray.
- The room bed renders order 0 (stereo), as decided in step 5; `room.bedOrder` is read by nobody yet.

Step 7, audio proofs
- `pnpm proof:audio` = `vitest run --config vitest.proof.config.ts`: `test-audio/audio.proof.ts` (Node) bundles `test-audio/page/proofs.ts` with Vite in memory, runs it as an inline script in headless system Chrome through Playwright (as the simulator's harness does; Playwright 1.63 wants Chromium 1243, which is not installed, so its bundled browser is only the fallback), and writes `evidence/audio-proofs.json`. Audio only: no GPU lock. `PROOFS=name,name` runs some; `LISTEN=1` also renders a 12 s ring flight per theme to `.cache/listen/<theme>.wav` (not committed).
- Each proof renders the real engine into an OfflineAudioContext (48 kHz), frames at 60 Hz through suspend/resume, and measures samples. Control = the same check on an engine broken by a test hook; the proof passes only when the control fails. Results on Chrome 156: direction 3.1 dB louder near ear overall, 18 dB above 2 kHz, the near ear 0.81 ms earlier below 800 Hz (control 0, 0.4, 0); pulse period error 0.02-0.1 % at time to collision 0.6, 1.2, 2, 3 s (control +15 %); peak -6.01 dBFS with master and every layer at maximum in a narrow corridor with everything sounding (control +11.8 dBFS); fade measured as the ratio to the same deterministic render without the fade: 0.007 in the first 50 ms, 0.51 at the middle, 1.0 after, on start and on a re-fade (control 1 throughout); at most 8 moving voices when 15 ask, hazards keep theirs, 32 panners in all (control 15 voices).
- `probe(what)` in the proof page is a diagnostic, not a proof: per-ear levels and the planned voices of one scene.

Step 8, lessons and runner
- Files: `lessons/<id>.json` (format `shramko-sonic-lesson/1`, canonical text, one per catalogue lesson, `order` = ladder index) and `lessons/paths.json` (`shramko-sonic-paths/1`: `full` = all 21 in ladder order; `fast` = ears-headphones, hover-height, walls-hall, corridor, rings-first, rings-course, free-garden; `fastPath` in each file agrees). `src/lesson/validate.ts` refuses unknown or missing keys, numbers out of range, lines that are not catalogue keys, a module-0 lesson with a world or flight goals, a flight lesson with question goals. `src/lesson/library.ts`: `getLesson(id)`, `lessonPaths()`, `lessonsOnPath(path)`, `nextLesson(id, path)`, `SHIPPED_LESSON_IDS`. Every lesson opens directly; the intro is `[intro.1..n, goal.1..m]`, so goal lines are spoken up front.
- Runner: `createLessonRunner(lesson, {locale, objects, start, floorY, progress, sessionStartMs, seed})` returns a `LessonSession` (contract LessonRunner plus `begin`, `takeActions`, `spoken`, `demoFrame`, `next`, `attempt`, `touches`). Host loop in the header of `src/lesson/runner.ts`: actions are `say` (engine.say, then `spoken(t)` when the speech queue is empty; otherwise the runner estimates 0.7 s + 65 ms per character), `cue` (engine.cue: gate notes, lap, checkpoint chime, reset sweep, fanfare, previews), `place` (move the craft by the respawn path: start or checkpoint), `ask` (digits 1..n, 0 skips; a question unanswered for 20 s is a skip), `scene` (module 0: feed `demoFrame(t)` to the engine instead of the craft while it is set). R maps to `reset('checkpoint' | 'start', t)` (start = the same attempt again, not a failure), N to `next(t)` (skips the intro, a question, the pause, a module-0 scene).
- Goal semantics (src/lesson/goals.ts, starting values): hold-height = a window of `seconds` from the first moment in the band, met at share >= shareMin; turn-to = heading within tolerance for 0.6 s (each attempt starts turned 0, 180, 90, -90 degrees); reach-zone = inside the zone (box in its own frame) and slower than stopBelowMps for 0.5 s, missed 2 m past it or after 3 s stopped outside; approach-stop = stopped (under 0.3 m/s for 0.5 s) within stopWithinM of the nearest wall (8 horizontal rays) or the object, after 2 m of flight, missed after 3 s stopped too far (shorter pauses to listen are allowed); around = 360 degrees around the object's axis; fly-distance = path flown in the attempt (cumulative over the lesson's goals; a jump over 3 m is a reset, not flight); pass-gates and timed-course = a forward crossing of the gate's plane inside its clear opening, a crossing within 2 m outside the frame is a near miss and fails the attempt; name-shape = asked after the gate, a skip counts, a wrong name misses; timed-course keeps `bestTimeMs`.
- Module 0 by goals: `locate` alone = the headphone check (left, right, behind; result good, swapped or unclear); `name-cues` with 0 trials = the glossary (every cue named, described and previewed) or, when every cue is a wall or a shape, narrated approaching scenes (walls from front, left, right, above, below); `name-cues` with trials and `locate` = the game (seeded rounds: a scene moving or still from left, right, front or behind; what, where, moving; feedback, a replay after a wrong name, the score). Met at 90 % (names) and 80 % (places).
- Pass rules: a block of `attempts`; passed at `required` successes (touches <= maxTouches), ends early once out of reach; blocking lessons pass after `blocks` passed blocks in a row, non-blocking ones (the game, shapes, free flight) are done after `blocks` blocks whatever the score. Lessons with only hold-height, approach-stop or around goals keep the craft where it is between attempts; the others place it at the start.
- free-garden is a scan lesson: the host passes `objects` (trackToObjects of `7a475d38-rings` v1 at the flown transform) and `start` (the track's start or the scene spawn); the lesson's own start is a placeholder.
- Locales: `quiz.option` {label, n}, `quiz.where-options`, `quiz.skip` added in all four languages (407 keys each).
- Fixed in step 4 code, found by the module-0 cross-check: approaching a wall on the left, the front and front-left sectors' slanted rays saw it with the same surface time to collision and outranked the left sector by their weight, so the wall sounded 20-25 degrees off. A ray now stretches its time to collision by 1 / cos^2 of its angle to the surface normal; the audio proofs were re-run on it and pass.

## Risks for the next agent

- The engine reads theme numbers by the names in `LAYER_PARAMS`. Ducking under speech cannot be proven offline (speechSynthesis is outside the graph): `duckDb` is unit-tested, the rest needs ears. With `selfVoice` off the engine ducks for an estimated speaking time.
- Lesson texts need the parameters named in test/locales.test.ts (`ARGUMENTS`).
- `tools/*.ts` cannot run under plain Node (extensionless imports, JSON imports): run them through vitest or a bundler; `tsx` is not a devDependency. Decide at step 11.
- This repository's tests (`test/`, Node only, about 1.5 s) are also globbed by the simulator's vitest when this is its submodule; they use only paths relative to the test file.
- The lesson texts make promises the lesson files must keep: corridor says a touch sends the craft back to the last checkpoint (assist `onTouch: 'bump-recentre'`), walls-hall says a touch is only a soft bump (`'bump'`), the ears lessons have no world (`{ kind: 'none' }`), hover-height.goal.1 needs `seconds` and move-stop.intro.1 needs `metres`.
- The formatter relies on `Intl.PluralRules` and `Intl.NumberFormat` (CLDR data): fine in every browser and in Node with full ICU (Node 22 has it).
- All thresholds in `DEFAULT_SAMPLER_CONFIG`, the theme numbers and the calm multipliers are starting values for tests with people, not findings.
- The lesson set (21 lessons, ids in `src/i18n/catalog.ts`) is my reading of spec section 3; a lesson is added or dropped only together with its keys in all four locale files.
- Stand-ins to revisit with pilots: walls-row uses fly-distance 22 m for "through the row" (the pillars generator has no zone to stop in); the corridor's goals are 50 m and 240 m of path, not progress along the axis; every threshold in goals.ts and the pass rules are starting values.
- The host must feed `demoFrame(t)` to the engine while a module-0 scene runs, and call `spoken(t)` when its speech queue empties, or the runner falls back on estimated speech times.
