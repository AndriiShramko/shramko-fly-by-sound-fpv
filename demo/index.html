Fly by Sound

A stand-alone demo of the first lessons, and the Sound Lab. Everything works with the keyboard alone. Wired headphones are best, and the volume should start low.

Start the sound

A browser plays sound only after you ask for it. When you press the button the sound fades in over one second, and it never goes above a fixed ceiling. Turn the volume of your device down first, then up until the speech is comfortable. Loud sound can harm hearing.

Flight demo

Pick a lesson. It speaks its instructions; press N to skip to the flying at once. A sighted helper can follow the readout and the captions.

Keys
  • W and S: forward and back. A and D: left and right.
  • Up and down arrows: climb and descend. Left and right arrows: turn.
  • N: next step, which skips the speech. R: back to the last checkpoint. Shift and R: back to the start of the lesson.
  • 1 to 9: answer a question. 0 skips it.
  • P: pause and go on.
